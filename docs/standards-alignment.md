# Standards alignment

> **Standards alignment.** Structured after, not compliant with, the GMLP guiding principles, TRIPOD+AI and the IMDRF SaMD clinical evaluation framework. This model is a research prototype and **is not for clinical use**: it must not be used to diagnose, screen or triage patients. Numbers are generated by `scripts/build_docs.py` from committed evidence files (named beside each number) and are not edited by hand.

Each item is mapped to evidence in this repository or to a stated gap.

## Good Machine Learning Practice: 10 guiding principles (FDA, Health Canada, MHRA, 2021)
| # | Principle | Evidence or gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Multi-disciplinary expertise is leveraged throughout the total product life cycle | Gap: a solo project; no clinical or regulatory reviewers. |
| 2 | Good software engineering and security practices are implemented | Typed code, tests and CI on every push; small commits; gates in the pipeline; `docs/silent-failures.md`. Security: gap until serving exists. |
| 3 | Clinical study participants and data sets are representative of the intended patient population | Partial: four clients and two held-out sites, per-site reporting; skin types IV to VI barely represented (gap). |
| 4 | Training data sets are independent of test sets | Lesion-level splits, gated (no lesion in two splits; frozen v1 splits); held-out sites never used in training or model choice. |
| 5 | Selected reference datasets are based upon best available methods | Partial: melanoma labels all histopathology-confirmed; benign labels partly confirmed without biopsy (`results/phase2/label_provenance/label_provenance.json`). |
| 6 | Model design is tailored to the available data and reflects the intended use of the device | Configuration chosen on validation by a pre-set rule; no clinical intended use (gap). |
| 7 | Focus is placed on the performance of the human-AI team | Gap: no reader or human-in-the-loop study. |
| 8 | Testing demonstrates device performance during clinically relevant conditions | Partial: external sites and a triage operating point; no prospective or clinical testing (gap). |
| 9 | Users are provided clear, essential information | `docs/model-card.md`. |
| 10 | Deployed models are monitored for performance and re-training risks are managed | Partial: drift detection measured in Phase 2; `docs/change-control-plan.md`; the monitoring job is Phase 3 (gap). |
